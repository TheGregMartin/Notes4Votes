End-of-round judging

First, set aside all Share tokens that were not claimed during the round. Remove the lid of the main tray (holding the central reference cards) and set it aside as well.

For each of the six a cappella groups, in alphabetical order from Group A to Group F:

  1. Separate the large piece of the group tray from the smaller piece and place it on the main tray (where its lid goes).
  2. Gather the number and colours of dice appearing on the group’s A and B cards (and, if appropriate, its Unexpected Change card) and roll them in the dice tray.
  3. Apply the Venue’s effect to the rolled dice.
  4. If the a cappella group has an Unexpected Change card that changes rolled dice, apply its effect.
  5. Each of the five judges then gives a score to that group:
    • For each die on the judge’s card, count the number of symbols showing on rolled dice of that colour. (If the same colour die appears twice on the judge’s card, double the count of that colour’s symbols.)
    • If the group has an Unexpected Change card that changes judges’ scoring, apply its effect.
    • Record that score, using Hits, on top of the Judge card.
  6. When all five judges have scored the group, combine that group’s Hits into a single overall score for that group:
    • Move all Hits on the Judge cards into the appropriate Hits token slots at the top of the main board.
    • From right to left, “unmake change” by replacing every pair of eighth-note tokens with a quarter-note token, then every pair of quarter-note tokens with a half-note token, and finally every pair of half-note tokens with a whole-note token. Tokens should always be placed in their own token slot.
  7. Place the Hits from the overall score in the right slot of the group tray, Remove the group tray from the main tray and attach it back to the corresponding smaller piece. Place the rolled dice back in the dice recesses of the main tray.

Tips: Assign each judge to a player, who carries out that judge’s scoring for all six a cappella groups. You can also assign a player to combine the judges’ Hits for a group and unmake change.

Once all six a cappella groups have been scored by the panel of judges, the group with the highest overall score wins 1st place, and the group with the next highest overall score wins 2nd place! Place the 1st and 2nd place tokens on the appropriate group trays, on the label in the upper left corner.

  • If two or more groups are tied with the same score, the tie is broken in favour of groups that are lowest down in the table. (Recency bias means that the judges might unconsciously favour the last groups to perform.)

All Content tokens corresponding to the 1st or 2nd place group earn Hits (from the general supply) for the players who possess them, according to the payoff numbers on the Content tokens. Each Share token on a Content token earns Hits for the player whose Share token it is, as if that player also possessed the Content token.
