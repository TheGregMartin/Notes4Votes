Getting ready for teaching or starting the game

  1. Shuffle the Venue deck. Place the face-down deck in the left slot of the main tray. Turn its top card face up on top of the deck.
  2. Place the central reference cards in the other slots of the main tray: all three Actions cards in the middle slot, and all three Payoffs cards in the right slot, both with Round 1 (Regionals) on top.
  3. For each of the six group trays, place the pile of Content cards (including a divider card) in the bottom left slot, in the following order:
    • 1st place: 6 Hits / 2nd place: 3 Hits
    • 1st place: 4 Hits / 2nd place: 2 Hits
    • 1st place: 3 Hits / 2nd place: 2 Hits
    • divider (a cappella logo on both sides)
    • 1st place: 16 Hits / 2nd place: 6 Hits (not used in this round)
  4. Shuffle the A deck and the B deck separately. In each group tray, deal one A card and one B card face up into the middle two slots. Place the remaining A and B decks to the left of the main tray.
  5. Shuffle the Judge deck. Deal one Judge card face up into the central red tray. Deal four more Judge cards face down into the other four red trays. Place the remaining Judge deck to the right of the main tray.
  6. Shuffle the Unexpected Change deck and deal three Unexpected Change cards face down to each player (who can look at their own cards anytime). Place the remaining Unexpected Change deck to the right of the main tray, below the Judge deck.
  7. Give each player 8 Hits to start the game (suggested breakdown: 1 half note, 1 quarter note, and 2 eighth notes).
  8. Give the Downbeat token to the player who has most recently been on a stage. They will take the first action of the game.
