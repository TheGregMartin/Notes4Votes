Moving to the 2nd or 3rd round

Discard all claimed Share tokens. Match all pairs of Share tokens and move them back into the dice tray

Discard all Hits tokens on the group trays back to the general supply. (Players do not discard their own Hits tokens!)

At this point, all players may discard any Unexpected Change card(s) they wish to. Then, all players (in any order) draw from the Unexpected Change deck until they are holding three cards. Discard all Unexpected Change cards that were played on group trays in the previous round.

Note: Whenever cards are discarded, they can be placed face down on the bottom of their respective decks; no separate discard piles or reshufflings are needed.

Discard the five Judge cards from the previous round. Deal five new Judge cards onto the red trays (one face-up judge in the centre, and four face-down judges in the other trays, just as with Round 1).

Discard the face-up Venue card, and turn the next Venue card face up on top of the Venue deck.

Construct the new lineup of a cappella groups as follows:

  • For the four groups who did not place 1st or 2nd, discard their A and B cards.
  • Move the A and B cards of the 1st place group into the Group F tray, and move the A and B cards of the 2nd place group into the Group E tray; they will perform last in the upcoming round.
  • Deal new A and B cards into the trays of Groups A, B, C, and D.
  • Remove the 1st and 2nd place tokens, placing them near the main tray.

Return all Content cards claimed during the previous round to the bottom-left slot of the appropriate group tray. In those slots, construct the small stacks of face-up Content cards for the upcoming round, with the highest payoffs on top.

Specifically, for Round 2 (Semifinals), place the pile of Content cards in the following order:

  • 1st place: 10 Hits / 2nd place: 4 Hits
  • 1st place: 8 Hits / 2nd place: 4 Hits
  • 1st place: 6 Hits / 2nd place: 3 Hits
  • divider (a cappella logo on both sides)
  • 1st place: 16 Hits / 2nd place: 6 Hits (not used in this round)

Note that the bottom two cards stay where they are from Round 1 to Round 2.

For Round 3 (Nationals), place the pile of Content cards in the following order:

  • 1st place: 16 Hits / 2nd place: 6 Hits
  • 1st place: 12 Hits / 2nd place: 5 Hits
  • 1st place: 10 Hits / 2nd place: 4 Hits
  • divider (a cappella logo on both sides)
  • 1st place: 4 Hits / 2nd place: 2 Hits (not used in this round)

The player to the left of the Cutoff token (who is the next player after the one who acted last in the previous round) turns it over to its Downbeat side and takes the first turn in the next round.
