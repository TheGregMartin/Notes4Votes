Setting up the play area

Construct the auditorium-shaped play area as follows.

  1. Set aside the lids of the yellow token trays.

  2. Set aside the lids of the red card storage trays, turn them upside down to remove their contents, and keep them upside down.
  3. Use the small double-hex connectors to connect the red and yellow trays in the configuration shown. The yellow token trays should contain, from left to right: whole notes, half notes, quarter notes, and eighth notes.
  4. Place the dice tray in the centre of the arc formed by the red and yellow trays.
  5. Turn the main green tray upside down and lift it to remove its lid and contents. Turn the empty main tray back right side up.
  6. Match the pairs of Share tokens. Place, inside the dice tray, a number of share token pairs equal to the number of players; return the unused pairs to their storage recesses inside the main tray. Note: the specific numbers on the Share tokens never matter—they just show which large and small pieces correspond to each other.
  7. Put the lid back on the main tray, and place the main tray near the dice tray as shown.
  8. Place the dice in the front recesses of the main tray, 6 dice per tray, separated by colour.
  9. Place the 1st and 2nd place tokens near the main tray for later use.
  10. Match each group tray with a smaller piece (A through F), and arrange them in an arc around the other trays.
  11. Distribute the four two-sided Player reference cards to the four corners of the table, or wherever they are most convenient for players to use.
  12. Players only need enough room in front of them for a hand of three cards and several small square Hits tokens.
